# Replacement asteroids

The project owner confirmed on 2026-10-26 that they created the replacement
asteroid models and textures. The existing OBJ LODs, PNG source textures and
DDS material maps are preserved without changing their geometry or appearance.
The release profile inventories these as `owner-created-asteroid`, separately
from restored Classic textures and from Remastered data imported at setup.
No public-domain designation or new license is applied by the packaging tools.
