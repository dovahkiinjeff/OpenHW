Rebuilds skirmish difficulty settings around mixed fleets, scouting and sustained pressure.

- Easy provides more time between assaults, smaller production commitments and slower expansion.
- Normal uses mixed forces, scouting-based mission preferences and regular attacks.
- Hard increases counter-building pressure, coordinated assault frequency and production expansion.
- Expert uses the full ship roster, faster strategic pressure and research priorities that respond to observed enemy fleets. The former restricted fleet list and cost/time bonuses are removed.
- Fixes AI diplomacy breaking assigned teams, first proximity sensors never being requested, and invalid resource-controller destinations.
- Reserves production-expansion funds while allowing essential economic purchases and urgent defense. All eligible carriers remain available for ship production.
- Shortens excessive assault staging delays and bounds failed attack-plan searches.

Gameplay balance testing is being performed by the community. AI laboratory tools and logs are not included in game downloads.
